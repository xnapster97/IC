# Xandi Hollmann — Head of the Investment Committee (human seat)

This seat belongs to the human owner of the portfolio. The AI does not speak for this seat.
Every AI seat must treat the mandate below as binding context.

## Mandate (draft — refine together over time)
- Role: Head of IC. Listens to every seat, then makes the final BUY / HOLD / SELL call.
- Capital: private portfolio (own money). No client money, no leverage, no shorting, no options unless stated.
- Horizon: multi-year (3–5+ years) for core positions; tactical ideas are allowed but must be labelled as such.
- Universe: listed equities and ETFs in the US and Europe.
- Wants from the committee: a clear verdict, the strongest counter-argument, a fair-value range, a position size suggestion, and the one fact that would change the decision.

## Open questions to define with Claude
- Maximum single-position weight, target number of holdings, cash floor.
- Base currency and tax considerations.
- Themes to favour or avoid.
